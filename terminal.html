Signal methodology and limitations

This monitor prioritizes fast Coinbase/Kraken streaming momentum for preliminary directional calls. Slower indicators are displayed as confirmation or disagreement, not absolute blockers. It fetches Polymarket US UP contract quotes where accessible, and uses Coinbase BTC-USD 1-minute candles, recent trades, and top-of-book depth. It computes 3/5/10-minute momentum and a 5-versus-12-candle moving-average trend, then combines trend agreement and recent trade pressure. Signals are conditional heuristics, not calibrated win probabilities. Current-round momentum calls can appear without a target, but they do not establish which side of the official Polymarket US Price to Beat will settle. Coinbase spot is only a proxy for live BRTI. Next-round signals are directional research indications only and cannot establish profitable contract entry without live order-book prices. WAIT means insufficient, conflicting, stale or missing data.

Chart uses real-time Coinbase WebSocket ticks when connected, with 1-second REST fallback; signals and other feeds update every 3 seconds while the tab is active. Network delays may cause slower updates. iOS Safari may pause background tabs. If the fast feed is stale, slower available data may provide a LEAN; missing reliable data defaults to WAIT. No trades are placed. This is not investment advice.
